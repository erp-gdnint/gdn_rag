GDAC MENTORSHIP PROGRAMME

Mentorship meeting 1 agenda

This general agenda is meant to help you ensure that you cover some key topics during your first meeting between mentor and grantee.

Introductions

This is the beginning of a very important relationship that will extend over the course of this GDAC grant. While you might know each other (or of each other) already, this is an opportunity to get to know each other further. Here are some of the kinds of things you might want to share or ask each other about.

| Grantee (to share with mentor) | Mentor (to share with grantee) |
| :-: | :-: |
| Why did you pursue this grant? What do you most want to get out of this experience?  | Why are you interested to serve as a mentor? What have you gotten out of previous mentoring relationships?  |
| What are you most excited or nervous about?  | What are you most looking forward to learning about the grantee and their work?  |
| What are some of your strengths and where would you like to grow during this grantee journey? | What are some of your areas of expertise that the grantee could draw on? |

2. Project updates and current status

The grantee should provide a clear and accurate summary of the current status of the project, including progress with re-scaling the project (in the case of 2nd and 3rd place awards) as well as initial progress or planning for activities such as ethics reviews, etc., if needed.

3. Developing your communication plan

A key outcome of this meeting is a draft of your joint communication plan, using [this template](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1S980edDezuvBCDKdc1FibG5yNIe1kFTC/edit?usp=sharing&ouid=116394735110013719331&rtpof=true&sd=true). Please go through the template together and discuss and at least make notes. The grantee should then circulate a draft plan shortly following this meeting for inputs from the mentor prior to both parties signing it and submitting it to GDN.

4. Next steps

Before closing the first meeting, you should discuss next steps and timelines for those. You will be working towards the month 1 deliverables, including a revised project plan and your communication plan. The grantee is responsible for drafting/revising these and circulating to the mentor for inputs in a timely manner.
