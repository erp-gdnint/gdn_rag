Standard Annex III of the 2025 GDAC grant agreements. Used for ORD, MIDP and JSDF grants

About this document

This is the standard reporting annex (Annex III) of GDAC grant agreements, as used in the 2025 edition. The same progress report template is used for the ORD, MIDP and JSDF tracks. The grantee’s own signed grant agreement, including its Annex II (dates and amounts of each tranche), is what applies to them; if it differs from this text, the signed agreement prevails.

Annex III: Reporting

The Recipient shall report to the GDN Senior Program Manager for GDAC (with a copy sent to awards@gdn.int), at the times specified in Annex II. Reporting for this grant will comprise the following items, which will be submitted:

Progress Reports as per the template below, with audio-visual evidence for each submitted report to complement the narrative.

A dissemination and follow-up plan for the project, focused on the project findings and achievements and on the next steps, considering the sustainability of the project.

A technical briefing of approximately 500 words about the project findings and eventual policy implications, for publication in GDN publications. This should be a one-pager summarising the project and its impact.

Additionally, a Progress Report will be submitted every 6 months of the project, regardless of tranche disbursements, to provide an update on the project. These reports do not require a Funds Utilization Statement (FUS). This is only required if the project has more than 12 months of implementation.

Supplemental obligations

The Recipient shall also ensure that:

Any change to the project, or to the team carrying out the project, is supported by a detailed justification and approved by GDN and the Advisor (mentor).

All documents and publications (including but not limited to research articles) published by the Recipient related to the project contain the following notice in a conspicuous place: “The project discussed in this publication has been supported by the Global Development Network (GDN) and Ministry of Finance, Government of Japan under the Global Development Awards Competition. The views expressed in this article are not necessarily those of GDN or Ministry of Finance, Government of Japan.”

The project webpage on its website, and any document published on the project, states in a conspicuous place: “If you have a grievance regarding the implementation of this project or towards its future impacts, send an email to awards@gdn.int, with a copy to fobino@gdn.int, or check www.gdn.int/awards/grievance for further instructions.”

In addition to reporting as per Annex II, the Recipient participates in monitoring and evaluation activities (including an Annual Survey) when undertaken by GDN, up to 2 calendar years after the conclusion of project implementation. When relevant and appropriate, the Recipient provides GDN with informal reports on project activities, and provides information to GDN when opportunities arise. This provision survives the termination of the agreement.

Progress report template

Please use this template for each of your reports. Each report must be approved by your Advisor (mentor) before submission to GDN.

1. Project identification details

Grantee (or associated institution)

Principal Investigator / Project Director

Title of the project being funded by GDN

Progress reported for the period

Person(s) who prepared this report

NB: this report needs to be read and validated by your Advisor before it is submitted to GDN for approval. Plan at least 2 weeks for this in your reporting timeline.

2. List of acronyms and abbreviations

3. Summary of activities and achievements/outputs produced

Please describe in a detailed way the activities implemented, and the status of the project, based on the original project approved and subsequent amendments. This can refer to the strengthening of your organisation, mobilisation of other resources, knowledge exchange, the creation of a new policy, replicable best practices, new products.

Please include as annexes to this report all available documentation for the activities described (for example: for publications, typically share the latest drafts available; for events and trainings, typically share the agenda, participant list, minutes or a report on the event, feedback surveys if applicable, pictures with captions, etc.; for recruitments, share the job posting and an overview of the selection process agreed; for procurement, document the procurement process, etc.).

4. Next steps (at least for the period between now and the due date of the next report)

Please let us know what is expected between now and the next report, directly related to the timeline, and whether you foresee any deviations.

5. Challenges faced and risks mitigated

Please let us know what internal and external challenges you faced during the reporting period, whether they relate to foreseen risks or not, and how you addressed them. We are particularly interested in understanding the nature of the challenges that emerge from the external environment, as well as any challenge related to internal operations. This will enable us to better support our grantees and improve our programmes.

6. Outreach and sustainability

Please let us know which external stakeholders you have reached out to for your project during the reporting period and how they can collaborate or work on follow-up on the grant outcomes. How likely, and for how long, will the outcomes be sustained? Are there factors such as technical, financial, economic, social, political or environmental that would hinder the scale of the impact in the future?

7. Replication

Please let us know to what extent the project has generated results and methodologies that are replicable in other sectors or regions.

8. Monitoring and evaluation

Please provide a version of the M\&E framework, with an update on the chosen indicators. Please mention any proposed change to the M\&E framework here.

9. Interaction and recommendations by the mentor

Please provide a summary of your interactions with your Advisor (mentor).

10. Recommendations for support from GDN

Please let us know how GDN could further support the project. Note that requests are discussed and accepted by GDN conditional on the availability of resources (financial and otherwise).

11. Budget narrative over the reporting period

Provide a narrative for the expenses reported in the submitted FUS. Please emphasise any under- or over-expenditure.

12. Proposed changes in the budget, workplan or team (if relevant)

Please present and justify any change regarding the budget, workplan or team compared to the previous information shared with GDN. Note that changes may need to be approved (over email, after discussion with the Advisor and your GDN counterpart) before they can be enacted. Specify whether changes have already been approved and mention the date of the communication with GDN about them.
